1. **Dual criterion for keeps** (philosophy-cycle precedent, decision log (5): subfield = recognized department/research-area level): a node must be (a) a major division in the classification sources (UDC 51/519.2, LCC QA, MSC 2020 top-level) **and** (b) present as a named research area/community at department level (journals, societies, department groups). MSC top-level status alone is a cross-check, not a gate.
2. **Refinements absorbed:** a node that names a refinement of a kept node serving the same community is dropped and recorded as a v2 re-split candidate (mirror of the movement-level deferral in philosophy: german-idealism).
3. **Cross-continent deferral rule:** a node is deferred to another continent only when **both** its primary LCC home **and** its dominant institutional home are outside the formal sciences. MSC/LCC-QA-filed boundary areas stay, with the boundary flagged in `uncertainty`.
4. Identity judgments use sitelinks+aliases, never English labels alone (decision log (9)); all QIDs remain unverified hints until the resolver pass.
