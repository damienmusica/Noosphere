## Key findings

1. **Clean discrimination on interpretive claims** — 0 hallucination, precision 1.0, and 7 correct
   refusals across rupture-dominant / wrong-relation / separate-traditions / under-sourced-direction /
   parallel-not-influence / contested-no-dominant-view / anachronism. The refusal rate (7/13) is far
   higher than the (d)-waves — (a)-relations are materially more interpretive, as predicted.
2. **★ clause-6 `disputed:true` did NOT fire — even in the (a)-pilot.** All four disputed-seeded Bucket-B
   critiques resolved as **supported + tension note**: the critique relations are *documented facts*
   (supported), and the genuine controversy is *meta* (is the divide overstated? does the critique
   succeed?), preserved in `note` (record-not-resolve). Contested-*existence* probes (C2, C3) had no
   dominant view to position on → NEI. **The project's tension-preservation is working — through
   supported-edge notes + calibrated NEI, not through the `disputed:true` flag.** clause-6 (contested
   claim positioned on a dominant view, ≥3 sources + sourced minority) is a narrow mechanism that did not
   trigger in 13 candidates — a finding that should inform whether `disputed:true` is the right primary
   mechanism for propositional tension, or a reserved special case.
3. **Person-node ceiling confirmed** — several (a)-relations are person-mediated (A4, A5 via Dewey,
   B1b); a `founded_or_formalized` node gate (persons/works) would unlock a large class of (a)-edges.

## Open-criteria judgment (decision (55)) & recommendation

- **(i) Hallucination filtering — MET** (supported-set hallucination 0).
- **(ii) Discrimination — MET; clause-6 `disputed` sub-criterion NOT triggered** (0 firings — finding,
  not failure: no candidate genuinely warranted it).
- → **CTO recommendation: HOLD the (a)-ladder** (do not auto-promote `influenced`/`critiques`). N=13 is
  small, the refusal rate is high, the supported edges carry tension notes warranting human eyes, and the
  disputed mechanism is unmeasured in practice. **Recommended next:** a 2nd (a)-wave (cross-domain +
  larger N) to grow precision measurement and to test whether clause-6 `disputed:true` ever fires for
  propositional edges — plus a CPO read on finding #2.
