1. **Person-mediated (a) works at precision 1.0.** 5 of 8 supported edges are person-mediated
   (person→person A1/A2/A5, person→field A6/A7), all with ≥2 independent claim-stating live sources. The
   #29 person-node ceiling is lifted; the pipeline handles person-mediated influence/critique without
   hallucination (0/16) and with disciplined direction/anachronism checks (E1, E2, C2 rejected).
2. **Cross-domain holds.** A7 (evolutionary biology→evolutionary psychology), B1 (→psychology), B2
   (information theory→cognitive psychology) cross domain cleanly; B3 (genetics→evolutionary biology)
   distinguishes a *datable historical influence* from a *constitutive/formal* dependency (D2
   probability→statistics, correctly NEI'd as `formalizes` territory).
3. **★★ clause-6 `disputed:true` did NOT fire — even under active hunting (0/16; cumulative 0/29).**
   Both Bucket-C disputed-stress probes resolved away from the flag: C1→NEI (no dominant-affirming
   view), C2→reject (dominant view denies the influence). The flag needs a *dominant view affirming the
   influence* **plus** a *live sourced minority* — a shape real controversies don't present. Tension is
   preserved through supported-edge notes + calibrated NEI/reject. This is now confirmed at N=29 with the
   broader candidate space #29 lacked. **Strong confirmation of #29 finding #2.**
4. **Untrusted generation, decorrelated QC.** The Sonnet layer produced a fabricated anchor (B2
   "Shannon"), a non-existent source (A1 "SEP Durkheim"), and an apocryphal quote (A6 "disease") — all
   caught by independent re-grounding. The pipeline's error-decorrelation is doing real work.
